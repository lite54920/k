# Deepstar Hearts

A 2D romance hunter game inspired by the gameplay of otome action games such as Love and Deepspace, with original characters and art.

Open `game/index.html` in a browser to play. It's a single file with no build step, and progress saves in the browser's local storage.

## What's in it

- **Four companions**: Orion (Deepspace Hunter), Lucien (cardiac surgeon), Marin (painter from a sunken city) and Kade (boss of the undercity), all drawn in code.
- **Stories**: three bond chapters per companion, with dialogue choices that raise Bond. Later chapters unlock at Bond 30 and 70.
- **Hunt**: top-down arena combat over 6 zones with 2 boss fights. You get a 3-hit combo, a dash with Perfect Dodge (slows time and charges your skill), and a Resonance skill that's different for each companion.
- **Wish**: memory-card draws (3★/4★/5★) with pity. Equip a companion's memory to boost your stats when you hunt with them.

## Controls

- Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys to move, J or click to attack, K / Shift / Space to dash, L or E for the skill, Esc to pause.
- Touch: drag on the left side to move, and use the on-screen buttons to attack, dash and use your skill.
